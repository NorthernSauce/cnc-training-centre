## The problem

### You've got the machines. You just haven't got anyone who can use them properly.

The kit cost more than most people's houses. And the operators learned from whoever was free on their first day, or from a manual nobody's opened since 2014, or from YouTube videos that contradict each other every third clip. The machines work. They just don't work like they should.

The training options didn't help either. The OEMs charge double what you'd expect, lock you to their brand only, and follow a syllabus that's nothing to do with your actual jobs. The internet's got everything and nothing. Hours of free content, no structure, nobody to ask when something doesn't make sense.

So it stays on the list. Another quarter goes by. The machines keep running slow.

## The plan

### Three steps. No fixed agenda. Your team back on the floor ready to use what they learned.

**01. Tell me what you need.**
Call David, or fill in the enquiry form. Describe your machines, your team's current level, what you need them to be able to do. Honest assessment in plain English.

**02. I build training around your work.**
Onsite on your kit. Classroom on simulators in Leicestershire. Programming services if you'd rather I just did the work. No two engagements look the same. Your situation drives the agenda, not a syllabus.

**03. Your team walks away capable.**
Operators who can set the machine. Setters who can programme the job. Certified, with the skills to apply on the next part that hits the floor. Not theory they'll have forgotten by Friday.
