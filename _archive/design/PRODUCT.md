# CNC Training Centre Product Context

## Register
Brand website for a specialist CNC training and consultancy business. The design itself must establish credibility quickly, while making it easier to find services and learning content.

## Business
CNC Machine Training Limited, trading as CNC Training Centre. David has around 30 years of experience and trains people on CNC machines, CNC programming, Edgecam and process improvement.

## Audience
Two equal segments: (1) engineering companies — workshop managers dealing with staff shortages, low productivity or idle equipment; (2) individual machinists — operators wanting to become programmers, career-changers, people who learned on the job and know they're only using 30% of the control. Best-fit company clients are organised shops with real machines and a genuine skills gap. Best-fit individuals are motivated and realistic about the work involved.

## Qualification role
David gets ~10 enquiries a day. 90% aren't suitable. The site must help wrong-fit visitors (wrong scope, wrong budget, wanting free advice) filter themselves out before they pick up the phone, while making right-fit visitors more confident to get in touch.

## Core Services
- Onsite CNC training at the client's facility.
- Simulator-led classroom CNC courses for milling and turning.
- Edgecam classroom training.
- Sub-contract CNC programming using Edgecam.
- Onsite productivity consultancy.
- Online training, planned for future release.

## Proof
- 30 five-star Google reviews.
- 27k LinkedIn followers.
- YouTube channel with 8.93k subscribers and 332 videos.
- Known by OEMs.
- Recognised online because of articles and practical CNC writing.

## Voice
Warm, approachable, direct and no-nonsense. Premium and polished, but never corporate. David's edge is practical authority and plain speaking.

## Primary Actions
Call David, email David, submit an enquiry for training or consultancy.

## Strategic Principle
The old site mixes services, machine controls, applications and blog posts together. The redesign must separate commercial services from Learning Hub content, then let users narrow by control, machine type or topic.
